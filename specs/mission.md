# Mission

## What
A static, publicly-hosted website for the **CSGRAD association at UTSA** (The University of Texas at San Antonio).

## Who it's for
Current and prospective CS graduate students at UTSA who need a central, authoritative hub for association information.

## Problem it solves
There is no dedicated online presence for CSGRAD UTSA. Students lack a single place to learn about the association, find out who leads it, get answers to common questions, and access graduate school resources.

## Goal / Deliverable
A clean, informational brochure site with four distinct pages — **About, Officers, FAQ, and Resources** — deployed on GitHub Pages under the [`csgradofficial-sys`](https://github.com/csgradofficial-sys) organization account.

## Success Criteria
- All four pages are live and publicly accessible via GitHub Pages
- Site is visually consistent with UTSA official branding (colors, typography)
- Navigation links in the top menu correctly route to each dedicated page
- Logos (CSGRAD and UTSA) are displayed correctly
- Site is mobile-responsive
- No external backend or database required — fully static

## Non-negotiable Requirements
- Hosted on GitHub Pages (no other hosting)
- UTSA official color palette (orange and navy blue)
- Fonts matching the style of bouldercsgrads.org
- Logo assets sourced from `logos/` folder (csgradlogo.jpeg, UT-sananotonio-logo.webp)
- Four pages: About, Officers, FAQ, Resources
