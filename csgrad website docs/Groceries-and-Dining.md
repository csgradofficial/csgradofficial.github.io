# Groceries and Dining

Listed for orientation only — CSGrad does not verify or endorse any business.

- A meal plan is only required if you live in UTSA Housing — see [Housing](Housing.md)
- One full grocery store is walkable from Main Campus; everything else needs a bus, bike or car — see [Transportation](Transportation.md)

## Roadrunner Pantry — Free Groceries

- Open to **all UTSA students, staff and faculty** with a valid UTSA ID, at no cost
- **Main Campus:** Student Union 1.04.06 · **Downtown:** Durango Building 1.306
- Non-perishable food and toiletries, order-ahead pickup, donated meal swipes, and monthly San Antonio Food Bank distributions
- Hours change each semester · [Roadrunner Pantry](https://www.utsa.edu/students/advocacy/pantry/)

## Campus Dining

- Commuters and University Oaks residents may buy the **Rowdy 75** plan
- **A GRA or GTA appointment does not qualify you for the employee dining plan** — student employees buy student meal plans
- **Dining Dollars** roll over fall to spring, then are forfeited at year end
- A campus nutritionist offers free consultations for allergies and dietary needs
- [Campus food locations](https://utsa.mydininghub.com/en/locations) · [meal plans](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/dining/meal-plans.html) · [downtown dining](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/dining/downtown-dining.html)

## Shopping Without a Car

- **The Runner does not serve grocery stores** — VIA buses do, free with the U-Pass
- **VIA Link** on-demand vans are free with the pass but only run within fixed zones — check the [coverage map](https://www.viainfo.net/link/) against the stores you plan to use
- H-E-B and Walmart offer curbside pickup and delivery
