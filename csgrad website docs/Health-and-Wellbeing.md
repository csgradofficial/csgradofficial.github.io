# Health and Well-Being

Informational only — CSGrad is not a healthcare provider, and none of this is medical advice. Every detail below changes; treat the linked UTSA pages as authoritative.

- Two separate systems: **campus care** (Wellness 360 — counseling and basic medical) and **insurance** (UTSHIP/AcademicBlue — everything off campus)
- Counseling at Wellness 360 is **free** to registered students, insurance or not
- **All in-person services are Main Campus, M–F 8am–5pm.** CS coursework is at San Pedro I/II Downtown — budget the trip, or use the 24/7 virtual options
- International students on F-1/F-2/J-1/J-2 are **auto-enrolled and billed** for UTSHIP unless a waiver is approved inside a narrow window

## Campus Care

- **Counseling** — free, in person or virtual · 210-458-4142 · RWC 1.500 · [services and how to book](https://www.utsa.edu/students/wellbeing/services/counseling.html)
- **Medical** — primary care, vaccinations, sexual health, diagnostics, run by UT Health San Antonio · [Wellness 360](https://wellness360.uthealthsa.org/wellness-360-at-utsa/) · [book via MyHealth](https://myhealth.utsa.edu)
- **TimelyCare** — 24/7 on-demand support, scheduled counseling, psychiatry, health coaching. Register with your UTSA email · [timelycare.com/utsa](https://timelycare.com/utsa/)
- **Anonymous self-screenings** — eCHECKUP and ScreenU, confidential, not linked to your identity · [screenings](https://www.utsa.edu/students/wellbeing/quick/screenings.html)
- Everything else: [UTSA Wellbeing hub](https://www.utsa.edu/students/wellbeing/) · 210-458-4142 · wellbeing.education@utsa.edu

Locations *as of Aug 2026 — verify*: Wellness 360 and Counseling **RWC 1.500** · Recovery Center **JPL 4.02.08** · PEACE **SU 1.02.22**. All Main Campus — no Downtown location is published. From San Pedro, use the free VIA U-Pass, routes 93/94 · [Transportation](Transportation.md)

## Health Insurance

**UTSHIP** = AcademicBlue, underwritten by BCBS of Texas. Medical, pharmacy, behavioral health, telehealth, and travel/evacuation coverage · [what's covered](https://utsa.myahpcare.com/benefits)

Auto-enrolled, billed to your student account:

- International students on **F-1, F-2, J-1, J-2**
- **Funded, full-time doctoral students with full graduate assistantships** — enrolled prepaid, single subscriber · [graduate student benefits](https://www.utsa.edu/people-excellence/current-employees/benefits/graduate-student-benefits.html)
- Master's students and partial appointments are **not** covered by that automatic doctoral enrollment — check your own status rather than assuming

## Waiving the Charge

- You are billed unless a waiver is **approved** — submitting is not enough
- Windows are narrow and open before the term starts (fall runs roughly mid-July to mid-September *as of Aug 2026 — verify*). Current dates and the comparable-coverage criteria: [waiver portal](https://utsa.myahpcare.com/waiver)
- Home-country and travel policies routinely fail on deductible and evacuation minimums — read the criteria before buying one
- An approved waiver lacking evacuation coverage still triggers separate AES enrollment
- International student questions: [ISSS health insurance](https://global.utsa.edu/students/health-insurance.html) · International.Services@utsa.edu · 210-458-7202
