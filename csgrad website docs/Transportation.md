# Transportation

Schedules, routes and rates change every semester — the linked Campus Services pages are authoritative.

- A car is not required, but going without one is inconvenient — transit covers campus commutes well, groceries and errands poorly
- CS grad work splits between **Main Campus** (NPB, library) and **Downtown** (San Pedro I & II). Three free services cover that geography and none covers all of it:

| Service | Covers |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| VIA U-Pass | All city buses, including Main ↔ Downtown |
| The Runner | Main Campus lots and a fixed list of nearby apartments |
| The Little Runner | Downtown Campus ↔ San Pedro I/II ↔ One Riverwalk ↔ Southwest |

- Set up the VIA U-Pass in your first week
- [Campus Services — Runner](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/runner/) · [Parking](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/parking/) · campusservices@utsa.edu · 210-458-7275

## Getting In from the Airport

SAT is about 15 miles from Main Campus. Rideshare from the Terminal A lower-level commercial curb is ~25 min; the GO Airport Shuttle desk at baggage claim is ~40 min; VIA routes 7 → 93 is cheapest at ~1 hr 20 min but involves a transfer — hard with a semester's luggage. Arrange late-night and weekend arrivals in advance · [SAT ground transportation](https://flysanantonio.com/home/ground-transportation/ride-share/)

## VIA U-Pass — Free City Buses

- Free to all current students — no fee, no application. Set up in the **VIA goMobile+** app with your UTSA email
- A physical sticker is available from Campus Services (MS 1.01.52) as a backup for when the app fails
- **Route 93 (Roadrunner Express)** is the Main ↔ Downtown connection
- **VIA Link** on-demand vans are also free with the pass, within zones · [coverage map](https://www.viainfo.net/link/)
- [VIA options at UTSA](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/runner/via.html)

## The Runner — Main Campus Only

Free, no permit. Does not go downtown.

| Route | Serves | Drop-off |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| 12/14 | Hill Country Place, Broadstone Ranch, The Oasis | Bauerle Lot 1 |
| 20/22 | Villas at Babcock, The Reserve, Campus Edge on UTSA Blvd, Avalon Place | Ford Lot |
| 45/47 | High View Place, Maverick Creek, Lark San Antonio, Tetro Village | Campus Oval |

- On-campus lot routes 13 and 43 run **fall and spring only**
- Live tracking: [therunner.utsa.edu](https://therunner.utsa.edu/) · [routes and schedules](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/runner/schedules.html)

## The Little Runner — Downtown and San Pedro

- The service that matters if your classes are in San Pedro I or II
- Free micro-transit, university community only, connecting Downtown Campus, San Pedro I/II, One Riverwalk Place and Southwest Campus
- [Downtown parking and transportation](https://www.utsa.edu/about/campuses/downtown-experience/parking-transportation/)

## Bikes and Scooters

- **Bikes must be registered with Campus Services** — free · [register](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/runner/bike.html). Racks on both campuses
- **UTSA Public Safety may provide a free lock** when you register a bike or scooter — ask when you register rather than buying one first *as of Aug 2026 — verify*
- Free self-service repairs at the **Tito Bradshaw Bicycle Repair Shop** (across from Ximenes Avenue Garage), and free semester-long bike rentals through Outdoor Pursuits · BikeShop@utsa.edu
- Lyft and Bird scooters operate on campus through their apps
- [Alternative transportation](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/runner/alt_transportation.html)

## Parking

- Grad students on a GRA/GTA appointment are **employees** and normally buy **Employee B**; without an appointment the equivalent is **Commuter Student (C)**. Employee B also parks in Employee A spaces evenings and weekends
- Buy through the **MyParking** portal · [permit types and rates](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/parking/permits.html)
- Where CS grads park: the lots beside NPB on Main; **D1 or D2** downtown, under the elevated highway, with the Little Runner covering the last leg to San Pedro · [downtown parking](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/parking/dtc-sp-parking.html)
- **No permit needed** 10pm–7am Mon–Thu and 4:30pm Friday to 7am Monday. Hourly parking via ParkMobile · [parking guide](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/parking/parking-guide.html)

## Driving in Texas

- Licence: [Texas DPS](https://www.dps.texas.gov/) · registration and annual inspection: [Texas DMV](https://www.txdmv.gov/)
- **Liability insurance is legally required.** International students should check with [ISSS](https://global.utsa.edu/students/) before applying for a licence — requirements depend on visa status and SSN
