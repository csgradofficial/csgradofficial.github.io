# Housing

UTSA does not verify or endorse off-campus listings, and neither does CSGrad. Rents and availability change constantly — the linked pages are authoritative.

## Before You Sign

CS graduate work is **split across two campuses**. Check your own course schedule and your advisor's lab location before committing to a lease.

| Location | Address | Campus | What's there |
| -------------------------- | --------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| San Pedro II | 622 Dolorosa St | Downtown | CS department office, faculty offices, classrooms, newer labs |
| San Pedro I | 506 Dolorosa St | Downtown | CAICC classrooms and collaboration space |
| North Paseo Building (NPB) | Main Campus | Main | Older CS research labs |

- **The Runner shuttle serves Main Campus only** — there is no Runner route between campuses. Downtown is reached on the free VIA U-Pass · [Transportation](Transportation.md)
- If your classes are downtown but your lab is in NPB, you will make that trip regularly — weigh both commutes
- [CS contact page](https://caicc.utsa.edu/computer-science/contact.html) · [Downtown at San Pedro](https://caicc.utsa.edu/downtown-at-san-pedro/)

## On-Campus

- Most CS grad students live **off campus** — halls are undergrad-oriented and grad students often need a manual portal override to apply (studenthousing@utsa.edu)
- **Every UTSA Housing resident must buy a meal plan.** None is selected by move-in, one is auto-assessed · [meal plans](https://www.utsa.edu/campusservices/dining/meal-plans.html)
- **University Oaks is the sole exception** — apartment-style, leased through Campus Living Villages, no meal plan required
- Main Campus halls: Alvarez, Blanco, Chaparral Village, Chisholm, Guadalupe, Laurel Village. Chisholm has private baths; Chaparral and Laurel are suite-style with kitchenettes
- **The Continental** (322 W Commerce St) is the only UTSA housing at the Downtown Campus
- Utilities and internet included; academic-year leases; billed to the student account, so financial aid applies
- Options and applications: [housing.utsa.edu](https://housing.utsa.edu/)
