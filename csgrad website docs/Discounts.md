# Discounts

Your UTSACard and your UTSA email are worth money. Most programs verify student status through your `.edu` address or a service like SheerID or UNiDAYS.

## UTSA Employee Discount Program

- Discounts with local and national vendors — arts and entertainment, automotive, child care, electronics, food, health and wellness, apartments and retail
- Redeem by showing a valid **UTSACard** in store, or entering a vendor code online
- [Employee Discount Program](https://www.utsa.edu/people-excellence/current-employees/benefits/edp.html)

## Retail and Subscriptions

| Service | Notes |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Amazon Prime Student](https://www.amazon.com/Amazon-Student/b?node=668781011) | Reduced Prime rate after a free trial |
| [Walmart+ Student](https://www.walmart.com/plus/student) | Reduced Walmart+ rate — delivery and pickup |
| [YouTube Premium Student](https://www.youtube.com/premium/student) | Reduced rate, includes YouTube Music |

- Student rates usually run for a limited number of years and require re-verification — check current terms before subscribing

## Software

- **GitHub Student Developer Pack** — free developer tools and cloud credits
- **JetBrains** — free student licenses for the full IDE suite
- Apple, Microsoft and Adobe all price education licenses separately
- Check what UTSA already licenses for you before paying for anything
