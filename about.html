About CSGRAD UTSA

Who We Are

The primary purpose of our initiative is to cultivate a nurturing and supportive environment for current Computer Science graduate students at the University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA). We are also dedicated to facilitating meaningful networking opportunities that bridge the gap between current students and alumni, fostering a sense of community and continuity. Additionally, we aim to provide academic skill development opportunities, and to equip our students with the necessary tools and support to thrive in their academic pursuits. Through these efforts, we aspire to enhance the overall educational experience of the Computer Science graduate student body.

What We Do

CSGRAD UTSA works to support its members through three core areas:

🏠

Community & Support

We create a welcoming space where CS graduate students can connect, share experiences, and support one another throughout their academic journey.

👥

Networking

We facilitate meaningful connections between current students and alumni, opening doors to mentorship, collaboration, and professional opportunities.

🏫

Academic Development

We organize skill-building events and workshops to equip students with the tools they need to excel in their research and coursework.

Bylaws

Officers

The officers of the organization shall be:

  • President Elected
  • Vice President Elected
  • Treasurer Elected
  • Public Relations Secretary Elected
  • Web Secretary Elected
  • Board Member Not Elected

The elected officers (President, Vice President, Treasurer, Public Relations Secretary, and Web Secretary) make up the Executive Board.

No committees are currently established. Committees may be formed as needed for special events or initiatives as directed by the Executive Board.

Responsibilities

Below are the specific responsibilities for each officer position.

President Elected

  • Directed the organization's strategy, planning, and daily operations
  • Managed projects, delegated responsibilities, and monitored progress
  • Organized and chaired meetings, preparing agendas and coordinating activities
  • Represented graduate students in departmental and university discussions
  • Led initiatives and events to support graduate student engagement and professional development

Vice President Elected

  • Assisted the President in organizational planning, operations, and decision-making
  • Organized meeting agendas, recorded minutes, and maintained official documents
  • Chaired CSGRAD meetings in the President's absence when needed
  • Managed email communications with members and the graduate student community
  • Supported the planning of events, documents, and organizational initiatives

Treasurer Elected

  • Managed the organization's budget, bookkeeping, and all financial transactions
  • Oversaw funding applications, expense reimbursements, and ensured responsible use of funds
  • Maintained detailed financial records and prepared regular budget and expenditure reports
  • Tracked event attendance, participation statistics, and financial outcomes to support planning and decision-making
  • Assisted the President with financial planning, reporting, and organizational operations

Public Relations Secretary Elected

  • Managed CSGRAD's social media accounts and public communications
  • Created and promoted event announcements and engaging digital content to increase awareness of CSGRAD and its activities
  • Captured photos and videos at events and maintained an organized archive of media assets for the Web Secretary
  • Developed communication materials to engage graduate students and the broader university community
  • Promoted the organization's mission, events, and achievements to enhance visibility, participation, and community engagement

Web Secretary Elected

  • Develop and maintain the CSGRAD website based on organizational needs and executive requests
  • Manage GitHub repositories, website source code, organizational digital documentation, and maintain comprehensive technical documentation including architecture, deployment procedures, maintenance guidelines, and transition materials for future officers
  • Publish and maintain event updates, news, galleries, and other website content in coordination with the Public Relations Secretary
  • Collaborate with the Public Relations Secretary to ensure timely and consistent communication across the website and social media platforms

Board Member Not Elected

  • Assist the executive team with planning and organizing CSGRAD events and activities
  • Support officers in completing operational tasks and organizational initiatives
  • Volunteer during events by helping with logistics, setup, registration, and coordination
  • Provide assistance wherever needed to ensure the smooth execution of CSGRAD projects and events
  • Contribute ideas and feedback to strengthen the organization's activities and student engagement
