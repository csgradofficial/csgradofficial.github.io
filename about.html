About CSGRAD UT San Antonio

Who We Are

The primary purpose of our initiative is to cultivate a nurturing and supportive environment for current Computer Science graduate students at the University of Texas at San Antonio. We are also dedicated to facilitating meaningful networking opportunities that bridge the gap between current students and alumni, fostering a sense of community and continuity. Additionally, we aim to provide academic skill development opportunities, and to equip our students with the necessary tools and support to thrive in their academic pursuits. Through these efforts, we aspire to enhance the overall educational experience of the Computer Science graduate student body.

What We Do

CSGRAD UT San Antonio works to support its members through three core areas:

🏠

Community & Support

We create a welcoming space where CS graduate students can connect, share experiences, and support one another throughout their academic journey.

👥

Networking

We facilitate meaningful connections between current students and alumni, opening doors to mentorship, collaboration, and professional opportunities.

🏫

Academic Development

We organize skill-building events and workshops to equip students with the tools they need to excel in their research and coursework.

Get Involved
