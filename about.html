About CSGRAD UTSA

Who We Are

The primary purpose of our initiative is to cultivate a nurturing and supportive environment for current Computer Science graduate students at the University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA). We are also dedicated to facilitating meaningful networking opportunities that bridge the gap between current students and alumni, fostering a sense of community and continuity. Additionally, we aim to provide academic skill development opportunities, and to equip our students with the necessary tools and support to thrive in their academic pursuits. Through these efforts, we aspire to enhance the overall educational experience of the Computer Science graduate student body.

What We Do

CSGRAD UTSA works to support its members through three core areas:

🏠

Community & Support

We create a welcoming space where CS graduate students can connect, share experiences, and support one another throughout their academic journey.

👥

Networking

We facilitate meaningful connections between current students and alumni, opening doors to mentorship, collaboration, and professional opportunities.

🏫

Academic Development

We organize skill-building events and workshops to equip students with the tools they need to excel in their research and coursework.

Bylaws

Officers

The officers of the organization shall be:

  • President Elected
  • Vice President Elected
  • Treasurer Elected
  • Public Relations Secretary Elected
  • Webmaster Elected
  • Board Member Not Elected

The elected officers (President, Vice President, Treasurer, Public Relations Secretary, and Webmaster) make up the Executive Board.

No committees are currently established. Committees may be formed as needed for special events or initiatives as directed by the Executive Board.

Responsibilities

The primary responsibility of each CSGRAD officer is event planning and execution, with some additional responsibilities specific to each position.

All Officers

  • Event planning and execution

President Elected

  • Schedule regular Officer meetings; create agendas, conduct meetings, and assign action items
  • Follow up with officers on action items
  • Appoint an officer to "own" each activity
  • Help each event's owner (purchasing, location, etc.)
  • Own CSGRAD events (approximately once per year)

Vice President Elected

  • When the President is absent or when asked: schedule meetings, create agendas, conduct meetings, and assign action items
  • Help each event's owner (purchasing, location, etc.)
  • Own CSGRAD events (approximately once per year)

Treasurer Elected

  • Handle finances and budgeting
  • Handle emails (reimbursements, event announcements)
  • Miscellaneous activities (elections, etc.)
  • Manage the organization's funds through an approved off-campus banking institution
  • Own CSGRAD events (approximately once per year)

Public Relations Secretary Elected

  • Manage social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, and Discord)
  • Take photos at CSGRAD events
  • Create advertisement materials including flyers and banners
  • Own CSGRAD events (approximately once per year)

Webmaster Elected

  • Create and maintain a page for CSGRAD on the CS Department website
  • Organize and maintain CSGRAD documentation (currently on OneDrive)
  • Own CSGRAD events (approximately once per year)

Board Member Not Elected

  • Attend and contribute to regular Officer meetings
  • Event planning and execution
  • Minimum requirement: attend weekly Officer meeting once a month (4× per semester)
