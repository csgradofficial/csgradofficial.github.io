🏠 Living in San Antonio

Freeways and Traffic

Welcome to the "spaghetti bowl." Learning to drive in San Antonio is somewhat of a wild ride — be patient and always expect the unexpected. Rush hour runs from about 7 AM–9 AM and 3 PM–7 PM. The sweet spot for errands is between 9 AM and 3 PM.

Local pronunciation tips: Bexar County is pronounced "bear." Huebner is "Heebner." Boerne is "Bernie."

What is H-E-B?

Named after its founder Howard E. Butt, H-E-B is the go-to grocery store in Texas. Get the H-E-B app to build shopping lists and learn where everything is in the store.

Must-try items:

  • Fresh "Butter Tortillas" and Mixla Tortillas (bakery section)
  • Creamy Creations Ice Cream
  • "That Green Sauce" (salsa/dip)
  • Exotic Sweet Potato Chips
  • Fresh Salmon Burgers (seafood section)
  • H-E-B Sparkling Water and Bulk Spices (great value)
  • Apple & Gouda Chicken Sausages

SNAP (Food Benefits)

Apply for SNAP in Texas

Tips for applying:

  • The process can be slow — be prepared to fight, wait on hold, and ask to speak to a supervisor.
  • Loans do not count as income. Don't include loan money when reporting your assets.
  • Apply before your loan disbursements hit your account, if possible.
  • Car value has disqualified applicants — some people register cars under a family member's name.
  • Persistence pays off — keep calling.

Medicaid

Apply for Medicaid — apply early because benefits take a while to activate. Pregnant applicants may qualify faster.

Superior Health Plan bonuses (if you choose them for Medicaid):

  • Over-the-Counter Program — $25 quarterly at CVS.
  • Free 6-month YMCA membership for STAR members (birth–17) + one adult family member. Call 1-800-783-5386.
  • Full benefits list

VITA — Free Tax Preparation

Free tax filing for households earning $60,000 or less. vitasa.org

Franklin Lindsay Student Loans

Fixed-rate, low-interest loans for Texas college students.

  • Up to $12,000/year ($6,000/semester)
  • No application fees, no payments while in school, no interest accrual while in school
  • Interest begins 6 months after leaving school
  • Requires GPA ≥ 2.0 and 24+ credit hours
  • franklinlindsay.org
